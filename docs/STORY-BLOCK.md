# STORY BLOCK — resonance-sistrum

*Per the Story Block Standard (resonance-standards, STORY-BLOCK-TEMPLATE).*

## WHAT

The sovereign musician's instrument — capture, layer, and shape music
that does not exist yet, on your own device, with nothing leaving it. It
is the creator's half of what used to be one app; its sibling,
resonance-compass, stayed a media player for music you already hold the
rights to.

## HOW

Combined, not built from nothing — most of the instrument already
existed as standalone waters in resonance-awen: `the-recorder`,
`the-encoder`, `the-tuner`, `the-metronome`, `the-waveform`,
`the-moment-marks`, `the-player`, `the-release-model`. Founded with
`the-founding-ritual` against resonance-standards, body from the
resonance-echoes lineage.

## WHERE

Nearest neighbor and sibling: resonance-compass, from which it was
separated. The boundary is drawn on rights, not on taste — KP's line:
does this create NEW sound, or work with sound you already own?
Recording and the four-track live here; fragments stayed with the
Compass, a DJ's work on material you already own.

## WHY

The same complaints kept arriving in different clothes — a shared
transport asked to serve a listener and a creator at once, and no
reconciliation made it honest for both. KP saw it and said so:

> "we need to separate the resonance compass and musicians compass to make
> this right. maybe the musicians compass belongs in resonance-sistrum, the
> compass remains a media player of licensed materials the user holds
> rights to."

## INSPIRATION

The seed: Aethelred's vision for a musician's compass (~2026-07-08),
ruled by KP the next day to become Compass v3, commissioned 2026-07-18
by Fable — carrying an open naming question, "The Musician's Compass" vs
"Resonance Studio," unanswered for twenty-five days because it was never
a mode of the Compass and never a studio bolted to it; it needed its own
house.

The immediate spark: on the founding day, KP was doing the exact thing
this instrument is for — standing at a phone with a microphone, trying
to keep a take, testing a recorder that froze on save. The separation
was ruled in the middle of that.

## PROVENANCE

Named and ruled by KP ⚛ (Quantum Weaver), 2026-08-12 — the repo created
by their own hand at 15:40 UTC and founded to the standards the same
afternoon. The boundary is theirs, verbatim: "fragments will stay, not
recording. dj's may still wish to use the app."

## ETYMOLOGY

Sistrum — Latin, from Greek *seistron*, "that which is shaken," from
*seiō*, "I shake." KP's own definition, given verbatim when they named it:

> "A sacred percussive instrument historically shaken in the worship of
> the Egyptian goddess Isis or Hathor. Its rhythmic rattling was believed
> to manipulate energy and ward off negative spirits."

The name described what was already built: the manipulate-energy half
was already in v3's plan, organizing a setlist "by energy, key, tempo,
emotional arc"; the ward half is the sensory law the whole family is
built under. A compass orients you; a sistrum is held in the hand and
played.
